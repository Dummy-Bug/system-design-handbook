**Massive shift at 1700:**
- **GEOM jumps to 31% of math problems** (from 18% at 1600) — geometry is now THE dominant math topic. Grids with cake cuts, ant paths, plot/garden enclosures, circle/rectangle overlap, square fits, lattice-point reasoning.
- **MOD_ARITH rises to 18%** — counting problems with mod 10^9+7 become standard (count permutations, count placements, count paths).
- **BITMASK emerges as standalone** — n ≤ 16 problems with 2^n state DP.
- **CONTRIBUTION rises to 7%** — sum-over-substrings / sum-over-pairs problems become more frequent.
- **STARS_BARS appears** — multi-variable counting like distribute candies with bounds.
- **CATALAN appears** — *All Possible Full Binary Trees* (LC 894).

# Section 1 — Geometry (31% of math problems, NOW THE dominant topic)

## 1.1 Cake / grid cutting (cost optimization)

**Fact:** For independent horizontal/vertical cuts, sort by cost desc, alternate cuts; each cut's cost gets multiplied by (current count of pieces on the OTHER axis + 1).

**LC problems:**
- *Minimum Cost for Cutting Cake I/II* (LC 3218, 1654 / LC 3219, 1789) — same problem at two ratings

## 1.2 Squares / square subgrids

**LC problems:**
- *Maximum Side Length of a Square with Sum ≤ Threshold* (LC 1292, 1734) — prefix sum + binary search on side
- *Largest Magic Square* (LC 1895, 1781) — for each candidate square, check row/col/diag sums
- *Largest Plus Sign* (LC 764, 1753) — precompute arm lengths in 4 directions
- *Separate Squares I* (LC 3453, 1735) — binary search on y-coordinate
- *Maximum Area Rectangle With Point Constraints* (LC 3380, 1743) — axis-aligned rect with no interior points

## 1.3 Apples / garden / plot

**LC problems:**
- *Minimum Garden Perimeter to Collect Enough Apples* (LC 1954, 1758) — apple at (i,j) has |i|+|j| apples; closed form involves cubes of layers

**Fact:** Sum of `|i|+|j|` over an `n × n` plot centered at origin = `12 × Σ k²` for k=1..n/2.

## 1.4 Distance / movement

**LC problems:**
- *Minimum Cost Homecoming of a Robot* (LC 2087, 1743)
- *Maximum Sum Circular Subarray* (LC 918, 1777) — circular array as 2-segment problem
- *Minimum Moves to Capture The Queen* (LC 3001, 1796) — rook + bishop chess moves
- *Minimum Knight Moves* (LC 1197, 1722)

## 1.5 Polygon / shape

**LC problems:**
- *Circle and Rectangle Overlapping* (LC 1401, 1708) — closest point on rectangle to circle center
- *Count Unguarded Cells* (LC 2257, 1708)
- *Largest 1-Bordered Square* (LC 1139, 1744) — precompute consecutive-ones from each cell
- *Minimum Area Rectangle* (LC 939, 1752) — for each pair of points, check rectangle with vertical sides

## 1.6 Cyclic / circular structures

**LC problems:**
- *Cyclically Rotating a Grid* (LC 1914, 1766) — per layer, k % layer_size shifts
- *Minimum Moves to Balance Circular Array* (LC 3776, 1739)
- *Distinct Points Reachable After Substring Removal* (LC 3694, 1739) — coordinate parity

## 1.7 Trapezoids / quadrilaterals from points

**LC problems:**
- *Find the Number of Ways to Place People I* (LC 3025, 1707) — upper-left vs lower-right pair constraints
- *Number of Perfect Pairs* (LC 3649, 1715) — algebraic min/max conditions on |a-b|, |a+b|

# Section 8 — Bitmask DP (subset state) (6% of math problems)

**Fact:** For n ≤ ~20, can iterate over all `2^n` subsets. DP states = (subset, last). Transitions enumerate which element to add next.

- *Maximum Compatibility Score Sum* (LC 1947, 1704) — match m students to m mentors via bitmask
