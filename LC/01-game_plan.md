### DSA prep specifics
- Use **zerotrac** (https://zerotrac.github.io/leetcode_problem_rating/) — filter 1500-1700, frequency-sorted
- **Sunday LC contests are non-negotiable** — that's the only thing that moves rating
- Add **1 virtual contest mid-week** (Wed/Thu, 90 min, past contest 6+ months old) — 2 pressure reps/week beats 1
- Pramp / interviewing.io mocks monthly
- Don't grind more solved-at-home problems. Need reps under pressure.
- Log every contest in `wiki/LC/contest-log.md` — track which problems beat you and *why* (pattern? syntax? time?)
- Upsolve only the problems that beat you, Mon-Tue after contest. Don't re-solve what you already got.
