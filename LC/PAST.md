# Past — History Context

I was born in December 1997. Here is my history honestly:

**The early years — village school comfort zone:**
Till 10th standard I studied in a village school where the bar was low enough that studying for one month before finals would put me in the top 3. Never really tried harder than that — there was no reason to. Then switched to CBSE in 11th and applied the same approach. Scored 67% in PCM. Was probably bottom 3 in class. That was the first real shock — the world outside the village school was different.

**12th — the villain arc begins:**
Decided to actually study from 12th onwards. Studied almost every day. Started seeing results. And then something clicked — once I realized I could do it, the ambition exploded. Didn't want to just pass. Wanted rank 1 in all of CBSE India. Studied 24x7. Got 84%. The effort was real but the result didn't match the ambition — and that gap between "I can do this" and "I got this" has never fully closed.

**The GATE years, PUBG, and COVID:**
Started Ravinder Baby Ravula's GATE course from 2nd year. Studied seriously for 3 months — then discovered PUBG and stopped. Tried again in 4th year, same pattern — 3 months of serious prep, then PUBG again, then deferred to a drop year. Both times I appeared for GATE CSE I scored 30 — cleared but barely. The score was never the ceiling. The preparation was.

Planned a dedicated drop year for GATE with MadeEasy Delhi — had it all figured out. Then COVID hit before it could even start and shut everything down. That was the moment I let go of the IIT dream entirely.

**The drift years (2020-2023):**
Graduated B.Tech 2020 into a closed world. Enrolled in a 1-year ML postgraduate course — no placements came out of it. Then enrolled in Scaler (DSA + Spring Boot, LLD + HLD) — cost ₹3 lakhs out of pocket. Finally got placed as an intern at a startup in August 2023. Converted to full-time. By then: 3-year gap on paper.

**At the startup:**
Small 10-person team. Because of the scale, I explored breadth instead of depth — Java Spring Boot (1.6 years), GoLang (3 months), MCP server + LLM chatbot with Gemini (3 months), React + TypeScript + Tailwind frontend (3 months). Surface-level across the stack, no DevOps. Salary delays are a reality. But I stayed because I value ownership over stability.

**The pattern underneath all of this:**
Low stimulation → coasting. High stimulation → villain arc. This has been the cycle since 11th standard. When the environment is easy, effort disappears. When the gap between where I am and where I want to be becomes real, something switches on. The 3-year post-graduation drift followed the same pattern — external walls (COVID, market) combined with internal ones (PUBG, losing steam after 3 months). Each rebuild produced something more real than the last. The question that remains open is whether the villain arc can be sustained without needing a shock to trigger it.
