**Key shifts from Band 1700:**
- **MOD_ARITH surges to #1** (18% → 22%) — count permutations, paths, sequences mod 10^9+7. Often paired with PERM_COMB or matrix exp.
- **GEOM drops slightly from peak** (31% → 17%) — still very common but no longer dominant.

# Section 3 — Bitmask DP (14% of math problems, emerging central)

**Fact:** For n ≤ ~16-20, DP states are subsets of n elements. State = bitmask of "selected so far" or "visited so far". Transitions enumerate which element to include next.

## 3.1 Subset state DP

**LC problems:**
- *Sliding Puzzle* (LC 773, 1815) — encode board state as int
- *Minimum Number of Flips to Convert Binary Matrix* (LC 1284, 1810) — bitmask of grid state
- *Count Words Obtained After Adding a Letter* (LC 2135, 1828) — hashset of bitmasks
- *Unique Paths III* (LC 980, 1830) — DFS with visited bitmask
- *Maximum Points in an Archery Competition* (LC 2212, 1868) — enumerate `2^12 = 4096` Alice-strategy masks
- *Maximum Product of Two Palindromic Subsequences* (LC 2002, 1869) — for each mask, check if palindrome
- *Fair Distribution of Cookies* (LC 2305, 1886) — bitmask DP: state = subset of unassigned bags
- *Largest Magic Square* (LC 1255, see 1881)
- *Campus Bikes II* (LC 1066, 1885) — bitmask of assigned workers
- *Min Generations to Target Point* (LC 3923) — see 2.4
- *Count Connected Subgraphs with Even Node Sum* (LC 3910, 1859) — small-n subgraph enum

**Key takeaways across the bands now visible:**

1. **MOD_ARITH grows steadily with rating** — at Q4 difficulty it's the #1 math topic. Combinatorial counting under mod p becomes the standard form.

2. **GEOM has a peak at 1700** — at hard Q3 difficulty, geometry is dominant; at Q4 it drops back because the problems are now more about counting/state-space.

3. **BITMASK emerges at 1700 and becomes major at 1800** — `n ≤ 16-20` problems with subset DP are a defining feature of Q4-tier difficulty.

4. **Math fraction nearly doubles from 1100 to 1800** (35-39% → 47%). At higher ratings, problems lean more on math insights than pure algorithmic patterns.
