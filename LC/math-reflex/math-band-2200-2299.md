**3463 (Check If Digits Equal):** After one operation, digit i becomes (d[i] + d[i+1]) % 10. After n operations digit 0 becomes Σᵢ C(n, i) × d[i] mod 10. With n up to 10^5 and mod 10 = 2 × 5, you need C(n, i) mod 2 and mod 5 separately via Lucas' theorem: C(n, k) mod p = product of C(nᵢ, kᵢ) mod p where nᵢ, kᵢ are base-p digits.

**1569 (Reorder Array Same BST):** The root of the BST is always A[0]. Left subtree = elements < A[0], right subtree = elements > A[0]. Any permutation that keeps the relative order of left-subtree elements AND right-subtree elements among themselves is valid — but left and right can be freely interleaved. So valid count = C(L+R, L) × count(left) × count(right). Apply recursively.

**857 (Hire K Workers):** Sort all workers by their `wage/quality` ratio. Fix worker i as the captain (with the highest ratio in the group). Every other worker in the group must have ratio ≤ ratio[i], so take the K−1 workers with lowest quality from those to the left in the sorted order. Sliding window minimum-sum heap over quality values. Total cost = ratio[i] × sum_of_K_qualities.

**932 (Beautiful Array):** The key invariant: for no triple (A[i], A[k], A[j]) with i < k < j, A[k] = (A[i]+A[j])/2. Separate all odds from all evens: an odd can never be the average of an odd and even (parity mismatch). So take a beautiful array of size ⌈n/2⌉ (for odds) and ⌈n/2⌉ (for evens), scale them (2k−1 and 2k), and concatenate. Recursively construct.

**3102 (Minimize Manhattan Distances):** The key rotation: substitute u = x+y, v = x−y. Then |x₁−x₂| + |y₁−y₂| = max(|u₁−u₂|, |v₁−v₂|). The maximum Manhattan distance between any two points = max range in u-coordinates OR max range in v-coordinates. Remove each point and check if the max decreases.

| [Largest Component Size by Common Factor](https://leetcode.com/problems/largest-component-size-by-common-factor/) | 2272 | union-find connecting each number to its prime factors as intermediary nodes |

**2543 (Check Point Reachable):** Operations (x,y)→(x+y,y) and (x,y)→(x,x+y) preserve GCD up to a factor of 2 (since adding means GCD(a+b,b)=GCD(a,b)). Starting from (1,1), GCD=1. After doubling operations, GCD becomes a power of 2. Conclusion: (tx,ty) is reachable iff GCD(tx,ty) = 2^k for some k ≥ 0.

**1627 (Connectivity with Threshold):** For each prime p > threshold, every pair of multiples of p is connected. Use sieve of Eratosthenes: for each p > threshold, iterate through multiples 2p, 3p, ... and union them with p. O(max_val × log log max_val).

**952 (Largest Component Size):** Connect each number to an intermediate "prime node" for each of its prime factors. Any two numbers sharing a prime factor get connected via that prime node. The largest resulting component is the answer. Factorize each number in O(√n), then union-find.
