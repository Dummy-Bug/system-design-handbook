A **News Feed** is a continuously updating stream of content shown to a user, personalized based on their connections, interests, and behavior.

It is typically the **default landing page** of a social platform.

Examples:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- X (formerly Twitter)
- LinkedIn

---

## In Simple Terms

A News Feed answers:

> “What’s new and relevant to this user right now?”

It is not just a list of posts.
It is a **ranked, filtered, personalized stream**.

---

## Core Characteristics

### 1️⃣ Personalized

Each user sees a different feed.

Based on:

- Who they follow
- Past engagement
- Interests 
- Time decay
- Machine learning ranking

---

### 2️⃣ Continuously Updating

- New posts appear automatically.
- Older posts move down.
- Real-time or near real-time updates.

---

### 3️⃣ Ranked (Not Chronological Only)

Early systems were purely time-ordered.

Modern feeds are:

- Algorithmically ranked
- Optimized for engagement

Example factors:

- Like probability
- Comment probability
- Watch time
- Freshness
- Relationship strength

---

### 4️⃣ Infinite Scroll

Feeds are usually:

- Paginated
- Cursor-based
- Lazy-loaded

User scrolls → backend fetches next batch.
