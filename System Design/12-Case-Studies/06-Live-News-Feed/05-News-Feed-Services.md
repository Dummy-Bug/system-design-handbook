
### News Feed Generation Service

Feed is generated by aggregated posts from user's following ,friends , pages or groups.

now we can have two scenarios
1. Frequent user
	for Frequent users their feed will already be ready and stored inside news feed cache . Hence experience for such users would be seemless.
	
2. Occassional user
	for occassional users we will not be having pre generated feed for such type of users. Thus only when such users open tha app we have to call News Feed Service which will generate the feed in runtime.


A. so let's explore more on occassional user say OU1(occassional user 1) in order to generate feeds for such users first of all NFS(News Feed Service) will have to get all the user_ids from the *RELATIONSHIP* table who all have some kind of relationship with the OU1.

B. once we have the user_ids we will fetch the friends information of OU1 using these user_ids by querying into *USER_CACHE* and if information is not present in cache then query for *USER_DB* .

C. so once we know who all people OU1 follows or is friends with and when we have their profile information as well then we will fetch their top N posts from *POST_CACHE* and corresponding *POST_DB*

D. once we have top N posts from each of his friends we will call Ranking Service that would rank all these posts from his friends according to OA1's interest , preference and relevance.


E. Hence feed is ready for OA1 after all these steps and this feed would be stored inside *NEWS_FEED_CACHE* . As we decided we will pick top 500 posts according to rank.

### News Feed Publishing Service

we can show OA1 top say 100 post and if he has scrolles all of them we can fetch next 100 and so on till 500(as we have only 500 posts in-memory).once all these 500 posts have been exhausted then again the process is repeated and 500 posts(non duplicate) are ranked and stored in-memory.


we are storing for each user 500 posts in-memory which would become the bottleneck so we have to store these posts information for each user efficiently inside *NEWS_FEED_CACHE*.so what could be the design of such a cache ?

<Post_id,User_id> , so we will only store the mapping between the post and user in the *NEWS_FEED_CACHE*. so once feed has been generated it would only contain <p1,u1> meaning post with id p1 from user_id u1 . Now during publishing the system would retrieve all the post_ids(p1,p2..) from the *NEWS_FEED_CACHE* and for each post_id it will fetch the posts details from *POST_DB* or *POST_CACHE* (as News Feed Cache only contains post_Id nothing more) and hit the *BLOB* storage and then display the Feed for the user OU1 who follows or is friend of another user u1 , u2 ...

but OA1 just won't see the Posts only rather he will also see the details of user who has posted and also when he has posted and all. so we will have to interact with *USER_DB* as well.
