# Instagram Functional Requirements

## In Scope

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | Users can follow other users |
| 2 | Users can upload media — photos and short videos |
| 3 | Users can view their **home feed** — posts from people they follow, ranked by recency |
| 4 | Users can view the **Explore feed** — personalized content from accounts they do not follow |
| 5 | Users can create and view **Stories** — ephemeral content that disappears after 24 hours |
| 6 | Users can like and comment on posts |
| 7 | Users can view a profile page — a user's post grid and follower/following counts |

## Out of Scope

- Direct Messages (DMs)
- Private accounts and follow-request approval flows
- Reels transcoding pipeline and adaptive bitrate streaming
- Notifications system
- Search by hashtag or username

## Key Clarifications

**Photos or videos?** Both — photos and short videos are in scope. The transcoding pipeline (converting raw video into multiple resolutions for adaptive streaming) is out of scope at this level; we treat video as a media blob stored in object storage, same as photos.

**Home feed ordering** — chronological or ranked? For this design we use **recency-based ordering** (newest posts first). Algorithmic ranking (ML-based relevance scoring) is out of scope — it doesn't change the fan-out architecture and adds complexity without teaching anything new at SDE-2.

**Explore feed** — at SDE-2 we treat the Explore feed as a separate service that returns pre-computed recommendations. We design the infrastructure to serve it, not the ML model behind it.
